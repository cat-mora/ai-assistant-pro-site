About AI Assistant Pro

Built by a founder who's seen tradies lose work for the wrong reasons

Not bad work. Not bad prices. Just a missed call when they were under a house or up on a roof.

The Founder

Hi, I'm Cat

My full name is Cathryn Mora. I'm the founder of Kindred Systems Group, based in Australia.

I've spent a lot of time working with small trade businesses across the country, and the same story kept coming up. A job goes to someone else. The tradie finds out later that a customer rang, left a voicemail, and just called the next number on Google when nobody got back to them fast enough.

It's not a skills problem. It's a communication problem. And it's completely fixable.

The Problem

The missed call is costing you more than you think

When you're on tools, you can't answer your phone. That's just the reality of trade work. But customers don't wait. If they ring and get voicemail, most of them call the next tradie on the list and don't look back.

Slow follow-up has the same effect. If you're calling someone back at 7pm after a full day on site, the job is usually already gone. They've moved on.

The tradies I watched lose work weren't bad at their job. They were just unreachable at the moment it mattered.

"

I built AI Assistant Pro because I watched really good tradespeople lose work they deserved to win. Not because their pricing was off, not because their reviews were bad. They just couldn't answer the phone while they were under a house or on a roof doing the job they're brilliant at.

That felt like a problem worth solving.

Cathryn Mora, Founder
The Solution

A fully managed AI voice agent, set up for your business

AI Assistant Pro gives your business a voice agent that answers every call, sounds natural, and handles the conversation the way you'd want it handled.

It books jobs, takes enquiries, and follows up, so you never lose a lead while you're on site. It's not a bot that feels like a bot. It sounds like a real person, speaks in a natural Australian accent, and knows your business.

We handle all of the setup, training, and ongoing management. You don't need to learn any software. You just start getting fewer missed opportunities.
