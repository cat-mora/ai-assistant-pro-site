The real-world problem

The phone rings at the worst possible time.

The cost is bigger than a missed call. It is lost focus, wasted ad spend, slow follow-up, empty diary space and customers who move on before anyone speaks to them.

The plumber in the middle of the job.

He is halfway through a messy repair. The phone rings. To answer it properly, he has to stop, clean his hands, climb out, take the details and then get his head back into the work.

Ten minutes later, the call is over. The customer may or may not be a good fit. The job he was doing has lost momentum.

AI Assistant Pro answers, captures the details and sends the summary, so the job keeps moving and the caller gets a proper response.

The electrician who needs to concentrate.

Why team size affects the price

AI voice agents are not free to run. Every call, inbound or outbound, uses real infrastructure: AI processing time, voice generation and phone network costs. The bigger the team, the more customers in the system, the more calls the agent handles each day, and the higher those running costs are.

The base price for Level 3 and Level 4 covers a sole operator. If you have employees, there is a small monthly amount per person to cover the extra call volume they generate. This keeps the service running properly as the business grows, rather than degrading at higher volumes.

Think of it like a phone plan with a data allowance. A business with three technicians uses more than a sole operator, the same way a family uses more data than one person. The per-employee add-on reflects actual usage, not an arbitrary surcharge.
