Common objections and responses

"Will customers know it's AI?"
Most won't. Natural Australian accent. If they ask directly, the agent is honest. In practice, callers just want to book a job.
"What if it makes a mistake?"
It escalates anything it can't handle via SMS straight to you. You stay in control of complex or sensitive situations.
"Is it locked in?"
Month to month. Cancel with 5 days notice. No contracts.
"Is cold outbound calling legal?"
The rebook system only contacts your existing customers who are due for their next treatment. That's a legitimate business communication under ACMA guidelines. Not cold calling.
"The setup fee is expensive."
It's a one-off. Covers building your agent from scratch with your voice, your business rules, your booking flow. After that it's just the monthly.
"We already use voicemail."
More than 80% of callers who hit voicemail hang up and call a competitor. An AI agent has the conversation and captures the lead.
