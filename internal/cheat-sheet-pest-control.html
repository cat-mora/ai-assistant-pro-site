Common objections

"Will customers know it's AI?"
Yes — the agent declares it upfront: "I'm an AI but you can speak to me like a real person." Required by Australian law. Most callers are fine with it once the call goes smoothly.
"What if it makes a mistake?"
It escalates anything it can't handle via SMS. Urgent issues: client texts PAUSE to stop the agent, Cat fixes it, client texts RESUME. Cat manages all changes.
"Is it locked in?"
Month to month. Cancel at least 5 days before next billing date or next month is charged. E.g. billing date 25th = cancel by 20th.
"The setup fee is expensive."
One-off. Covers building the agent from scratch — custom voice, business rules, booking flow. After that it's just the monthly.
"We already use voicemail."
More than 80% of callers who hit voicemail hang up and call the next business. An AI agent has the conversation and captures the lead.
"Can it integrate with my booking system?"
L2 and above connect to calendar/booking system. Confirmed during setup. Common: Google Calendar, ServiceM8.
