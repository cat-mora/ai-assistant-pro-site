How clients control their agent (post go-live)

PAUSE / RESUME (all levels)

Client texts PAUSE or RESUME to +61 468 154 584 from their registered mobile.

PAUSE → calls go to voicemail / their number. Billing continues. No time limit.

RESUME → agent back on within minutes.

Urgent issue: client PAUSEs immediately, emails hello@ describing the problem. Cat fixes and confirms before RESUME.

Change requests (all levels)

Client emails hello@aiassistantpro.com.au

Subject format: CHANGE REQUEST — [Business Name] — [description]

2 changes included per month. Additional at A$95/hr.

Cat makes all changes to the agent in Vapi. Client never touches Vapi.

SLA: same business day acknowledge, next business day complete.

L4 — Rebook approval flow

Before any outbound calls run, client must approve the contact list.

Current process (manual):

  • Agent/Cat generates proposed list from client's customer records
  • Cat emails list to client for approval
  • Client replies: approved, or "remove Smith, Jones"
  • Cat updates list and runs the campaign

Planned Client portal app will automate this approval step.
