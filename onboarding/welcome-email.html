What to have ready for the intake call

You don't need to prep much. But having these handy will make the call quicker and smoother:

■ Your business name and the name you'd like the agent to use when answering calls
■ The services you offer (a rough list is fine, we'll refine it together)
■ Your service areas — which suburbs or regions you cover
■ Your operating hours and any after-hours rules (e.g. emergency callouts)
■ How you prefer leads to reach you (email, SMS, CRM — whatever you currently use)
■ Any common questions your customers ask — pricing, callout fees, wait times, etc.

A couple of things worth knowing

Your agent will be live within 48 hours of the intake call, in most cases. We work quickly because we know your time matters.

And nothing gets switched on until you've heard it and approved it. You're in control of the whole process.
