# Session 2H — YouTube Channel Setup + Anchor Videos

## What this session is for
Set up the AI Assistant Pro YouTube channel and produce three anchor proof videos — one per key service workflow. These videos become website proof assets, social clips, email content, sales follow-up material and partner assets.

## Context
Read COWORK-HANDOVER.md in this folder first.
DEPENDENCY: Social profiles set up in session 1O first.

## YouTube channel setup

- Channel name: AI Assistant Pro
- Handle: @aiassistantpro (or closest available)
- Profile image: AAP logo
- Banner: branded (navy/gold/cream, tagline)
- About section: 2–3 paragraphs from COWORK-HANDOVER.md
- Links: website, Linktree, booking page
- Channel trailer: short version of anchor video 1 (electrician demo)

## The three anchor videos

These are produced once and compound indefinitely. Cathryn records them — Claude writes the scripts, descriptions, titles and tags.

### Video 1 — Answering calls (L1/L2 proof)
**Title:** "Watch an AI receptionist handle a real electrician enquiry"
**Length:** 2–3 minutes
**Content:** Play a real (or realistic demo) call. Cathryn briefly introduces it, the call plays, Cathryn briefly explains what happened and what the business owner would have received. No jargon. Plain English.
**CTA:** Try the demo yourself → link to homepage
**Used in:** Homepage proof section, email Branch 1 + 3, social clips

### Video 2 — Lead callback (L3 proof)
**Title:** "Watch our AI call back a Facebook lead in 60 seconds"
**Length:** 2–3 minutes
**Content:** Show the full flow: form submitted → 60 seconds → AI calls → qualifies → books. Cathryn narrates briefly. Show the Stripe SMS if possible.
**CTA:** Check if Lead Callback suits your business → compatibility check link
**Used in:** /tradies page, email Branch 2, partner assets, social clips

### Video 3 — Reactivation (L4 proof)
**Title:** "Watch our AI reactivate an overdue pest control customer"
**Length:** 2–3 minutes
**Content:** Show the flow: customer list → AI identifies overdue → calls → books return visit. Cathryn narrates. Real or realistic demo call.
**CTA:** Check if Rebook & Reactivate suits your business → compatibility check link
**Used in:** Email Branch 4, pest control SEO page, partner assets, social clips
