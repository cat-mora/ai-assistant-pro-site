# Session 2I — Partner / Referral Program

## What this session is for
Build a simple partner program targeting people who already work with tradies and service businesses. One outreach session, then the program runs itself. Partners refer clients, earn commission, and you get warm leads from trusted sources.

## Context
Read COWORK-HANDOVER.md in this folder first.

## Target partner types (in priority order)

1. **ServiceM8, Tradify, Jobber consultants and resellers** — they implement software for tradies and constantly hear "I can't answer my phone" and "my leads go cold"
2. **Google Ads agencies serving trades** — they hear "we're paying for leads but they're not converting" every week
3. **Tradie web designers and lead-gen agencies** — build sites and funnels for tradies, often know when a client's follow-up is broken
4. **Business coaches for tradies** — work closely with owners on operations and revenue
5. **Managed IT and phone-system providers** — already touching the phone infrastructure
6. **Bookkeepers and accountants** — secondary, but they know when a tradie's revenue is flat

## What to build in this session

### 1. Partner landing page
URL: aiassistantpro.com.au/partners
Content:
- Who the program is for
- What AI Assistant Pro does (one paragraph)
- Who it suits best (referral guide — which clients to refer)
- Commission structure
- How it works (refer → client pays → you get paid)
- Apply / register CTA

### 2. Commission structure (decide in session, suggested starting point)
- 10–15% of first month's subscription, paid after 30 days
- Or: flat referral fee per converted client (e.g. $100–$200)
- Track via unique referral links (UTM or dedicated landing pages per partner)

### 3. Partner kit (one-pager)
A single PDF or web page partners can share with their clients:
- What AI Assistant Pro does
- Who it's best for
- Pricing overview
- Demo phone number (so partners can demo it themselves)
- "Referred by [Partner Name]" landing page link

### 4. Unique referral links / landing pages
One page per partner type (minimum):
- aiassistantpro.com.au/ref/servicem8
- aiassistantpro.com.au/ref/google-ads
- aiassistantpro.com.au/ref/[partner-name] for individual partners
Page personalised: "Referred by [Partner]" — makes landing page feel relevant, tracks source properly.

### 5. Outreach email templates
Short, personalised, useful — not spammy.
Pitch angle: "Your clients are probably losing jobs to missed calls or cold leads. Here's something that fixes that automatically."
Attach or link to partner kit.
Follow up once only.

### 6. Partner registration / tracking
Simple: Google Form or Typeform to register interest → added to a partner list in Supabase or Google Sheet → unique link generated → commission tracked in Stripe or manually.

## Outreach targets to identify in this session
- 5–10 ServiceM8/Tradify/Jobber consultants (find via their own marketplaces and directories)
- 5 Google Ads agencies with tradie clients (LinkedIn search)
- 5 tradie-focused web designers (Google search + Facebook Groups)
- 5 tradie business coaches (LinkedIn, podcast hosts, Facebook Group admins)

## Outputs expected
- Partner landing page live on website
- Commission structure decided and documented
- Partner kit (one-pager) created in Canva
- Unique referral links set up and tracking confirmed
- Outreach email template written
- First 20 outreach emails sent
- Partner registration form live
