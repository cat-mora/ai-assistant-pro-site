# Session 2J — Podcast + Webinar Outreach

## What this session is for
Identify AU tradie and small business podcasts and webinar opportunities, draft pitch emails, and send first round of outreach. One session, then compounds over time as appearances are booked.

## Context
Read COWORK-HANDOVER.md in this folder first.

## Pitch angle

Don't pitch "interview me about AI." Pitch a useful topic with a live demonstration.

**Strong pitch angles:**
- "Stop losing paid leads after 5pm — live demo included"
- "How a pest control business can automate its rebooking calls"
- "What happens when a tradie misses a call (and how to fix it automatically)"
- "AI phone agents for tradies — what's real and what's hype"
- "How to call back a web lead in 60 seconds automatically"

The pitch should offer value to their audience, not just exposure for AAP. Include an offer to demonstrate live on the episode.

## Target venues

### Podcasts (find and research in this session)
- AU tradie and trades business podcasts (e.g. Tradie Business School, Built to Last, similar)
- AU small business podcasts with tradie/service business audience
- Home services industry podcasts
- Business systems and automation podcasts

Identify 10 targets. For each: name, host, audience size estimate, contact email, best pitch angle.

### Webinars and online events
- Trade association webinars (Master Electricians Australia, MPAQ, AIRAH, pest control associations)
- ServiceM8 / Tradify / Jobber partner webinars or community events
- Google Ads agency client education sessions
- Business coach group training sessions

### Newsletters
- Tradie industry newsletters
- Small business AU newsletters
- ServiceM8 / Tradify partner newsletters

## Outreach email template

```
Subject: [Specific topic] — live demo for your [audience]

Hi [Name],

I listened to [specific episode / read your newsletter] and noticed your audience is largely [tradies / service business owners] — so I thought this might be relevant.

I run AI Assistant Pro, which helps service businesses stop losing jobs to missed calls and slow lead follow-up using AI phone agents.

I'd love to pitch a practical session for your audience: [specific topic — e.g. "How to call back a web lead in 60 seconds automatically"]. I can run a live demo on the call so they can hear what the technology actually sounds like.

No fluff — just a practical look at something that directly affects the money coming into a trades business.

Would this be a fit for [podcast/newsletter/webinar]?

Cathryn
AI Assistant Pro
aiassistantpro.com.au
```

Personalise each outreach — reference a specific episode or piece of content they've produced. Generic pitches don't work.

## Follow-up
One follow-up only, 7–10 days after first email if no response. Then stop.

## Tracking
Simple Google Sheet: Venue / Host / Contact / Date sent / Follow-up date / Status / Notes
